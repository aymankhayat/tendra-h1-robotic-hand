Built around the human hand

Real Anatomy.
Real Engineering.

Link lengths come from adult hand anthropometry. Joint limits follow human range of motion, with hyperextension capped at 5–15°. The thumb sits on its own CMC ball joint, so it truly opposes instead of acting as a fifth finger.

Grasp taxonomy

Six Grasps.
Zero Collisions.

Open, Fist, Pinch, Point, Power Grip and OK Sign come from robotic grasp research. The thumb's opposition angles were found by a forward-kinematics search, and every preset passes an automated BVH interference check against the real part geometry.

Case study

Engineering Notes

Read the full case study
01 · Kinematics
0 mm penetration

A thumb that really opposes

A forward-kinematics search over the CMC rotations replaced guesswork, then 441 configurations per grasp were checked for collision-free thumb values.

02 · Verification
64 → 0 false flags

A collision checker you can trust

Normal-based tests gave way to ray-parity voting per solid island. A known-bad pose still gets caught at 2.2 mm, and four real clashes were found and fixed.

03 · Web pipeline
8.5° → 85°

Blender drivers, rebuilt for glTF

Eleven controls were baked into separate clips. Stripping the static bone tracks the exporter adds fixed the clips averaging each other out.

04 · Rendering
100× light recalibration

Photoreal at true scale

A lights-off / lights ÷100 test isolated overexposure at real-world size. The HDRI was desaturated to reflections only, with a single emissive accent.
